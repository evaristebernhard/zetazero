\section{Reduced spectral space and parameter hierarchy}
\label{sec:retained}

We now choose the constants that were kept symbolic in the preceding analytic
estimates.  First fix
\[
0<C_{\rm pkt}<2.
\]
This determines the logarithmic packet reserve in
Section~\ref{sec:shell-frame}.  The upper bound is used only in the geometric
exterior row count of Proposition~\ref{prop:geometric-edge-ledger}.

Fix once and for all the finite derivative orders required for two curvature
derivatives and two primitive mixed derivatives.  Let $C_{\rm mean}$ dominate
the fixed shell/source multiplicity in the local mean constraints.  Let
$C_{\rm geom}$ dominate the fixed logarithmic losses in the stationary symbol,
geometric leakage and shell normalization, and let $C_{\rm par}$ dominate the
fixed losses in the finite-height, direct-residual and $f'$ profiles of
Section~\ref{sec:logonly-remainder-ledger}.

Choose a height partition scale
\[
H:=T/L^D
\]
with one fixed $D>C_{\rm geom}+5$.  For the scalar HLZ diagonal choose its
arbitrary saving exponent after all fixed Cauchy and packet losses: fix
\[
A_{\rm diag}>C_{\rm geom}+C_{\rm par}+10.
\]
Choose a nonstationary integration-by-parts order
\begin{equation}
\boxed{N\ge8}
\label{eq:nonstationary-order-choice}
\end{equation}
and then choose the stationary buffer exponent $B$ large enough to absorb the
fixed derivative losses in \eqref{eq:nonstat-shell}.  Finally choose $A_0$ so
large that
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
A_0>\max\{B+3,\ C_{\rm mean}+3,\ C_{\rm geom}+C_{\rm par}+5\}.
}
\label{eq:acyclic-A0-choice}
\end{equation}
All these constants are fixed before $T\to\infty$.  In particular the stable
core
\[
W_T=L-A_0\log L,
\qquad
X_T=\frac{T}{2\pi L^{A_0}}
\]
is exactly the one defined in \eqref{eq:stable-log-core-main}.

\begin{lemma}[Global and local mean constraints]
\label{lem:mean-zero-space}
Let $g(u;v)$, $0\le u\le W_T$, be the flat-core packet output immediately
before the global primitive and define
\begin{equation}
\mathcal M_T^{\rm glob}v:=\int_0^{W_T}g(u;v)\,du.
\label{eq:global-mean-map}
\end{equation}
For each retained shell and each fixed local output label $\alpha$, let
$q_{j,\alpha}(t;v)$ be the corresponding primitive input from
Section~\ref{sec:shell-frame}, and define
\begin{equation}
\mathcal M_T^{\rm loc}v
:=\left(\int_0^1q_{j,\alpha}(t;v)\,dt\right)_{j,\alpha}.
\label{eq:local-mean-map}
\end{equation}
Put
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
V_{\rm mean}:=\ker\mathcal M_T^{\rm glob}
\cap\ker\mathcal M_T^{\rm loc}.
}
\label{eq:mean-map}
\end{equation}
Then
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\codim V_{\rm mean}
\ll(1+X_T)L^{C_{\rm mean}}=o(N_T).
}
\label{eq:mean-codim}
\end{equation}
For every $v\in V_{\rm mean}$ the global primitive and every shell-local
primitive vanish at both endpoints, so both the global model identity and the
two-variable primitive criterion apply on the same subspace.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The global mean map has one output coordinate.  There are $O(X_T)$ retained
unit shells and at most $L^{C_{\rm mean}}$ fixed local output labels per shell,
so the target dimension of $\mathcal M_T^{\rm loc}$ is
$O(X_TL^{C_{\rm mean}})$.  The codimension is at most the sum of these target
dimensions.  By \eqref{eq:acyclic-A0-choice},
\[
X_TL^{C_{\rm mean}}
\ll TL^{-A_0+C_{\rm mean}}=o(TL)=o(N_T).
\]
The endpoint identities are exactly the defining kernel conditions.
\end{proof}

Let $P_{\rm end}$ be the coordinate projection deleting packet centres in the
two horizontal endpoint collars from Lemma~\ref{lem:horizontal-decay}, and put
\begin{equation}
V_{\rm end}:=\Ran P_{\rm end}.
\label{eq:end-space}
\end{equation}
Then
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\codim V_{\rm end}=O(T)=o(N_T).}
\label{eq:end-codim}
\end{equation}
This is a genuine packet-coordinate restriction.  The stationary buffer rows
are not deleted; Proposition~\ref{prop:geometric-edge-ledger} accounts for
their geometric contribution.

Define
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\Pret:=V_{\rm core}\cap V_{\rm mean}\cap V_{\rm end}.
}
\label{eq:retained-space}
\end{equation}
By Lemma~\ref{lem:stable-core-frame}, \eqref{eq:mean-codim}, and
\eqref{eq:end-codim},
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\codim\Pret=o(N_T).}
\label{eq:retained-codim}
\end{equation}
The log-only frozen model requires no additional spectral deletion by
Proposition~\ref{prop:model-floor}.  There is no critical-$J$ AFE deletion, no
frozen-self deletion, and no low-sideband principal-source deletion in the
revised hierarchy.
