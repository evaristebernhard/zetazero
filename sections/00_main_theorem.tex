\begin{abstract}
We construct a finite-dimensional Hermitian contour form from an analytic Hardy
gauge and a near-lattice packet family.  Its zero-side residue representation
converts off-critical-line stationary geometry into negative inertia.  On the
arithmetic side the holomorphic archimedean self block is eliminated on the
closed contour, and the complete non-$f'$ arithmetic source
$-P(s)+Q(s)/(f(s)-P(s))$ is kept inside the theorem-facing operator.  The
remaining $f'$ source is treated separately.  The true operator retains the stationary
additive carrier $e(-ne^d)$.  The untwisted scalar Heap--Li--Zhao diagonal does
not presently control this operator, so no unconditional packet--HLZ bridge is
claimed.  We instead prove a conditional density-one theorem.  If the negative
part of the relative defect between the true one-$\chi$ operator and a positive
reference model has normalized Hilbert--Schmidt square $O((\log T)^{-2})$, and
the geometric edge and regular remainders satisfy the stated rank and relative
Hilbert--Schmidt estimates, then
\[
\frac{N_{0,T}}{N_T}=1-O((\log T)^{-2})-o(1)=1-o(1).
\]
Thus the unresolved arithmetic content is isolated in one explicit one-sided
relative spectral-defect hypothesis.
\end{abstract}

\section{Main theorem and notation}

Let
\[
N_T:=N(T,2T)
\]
denote the number, with multiplicity, of nontrivial zeros of $\zeta$ with
imaginary part in $[T,2T]$, and let $N_{0,T}$ denote the number of those zeros
on the critical line. Thus $N_T\asymp T\log T$. Put
\[
L:=\log\frac{T}{2\pi}.
\]
Throughout the paper the certified reference-floor constant is
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\kappa_*:=0.02.}
\label{eq:global-kappa}
\end{equation}

For a Hermitian operator $B$, write $B_-=(-B)_+$ for its negative part.  Let
$A_T^{\rm true}$ be the retained log-only Hermitian operator defined in
Section~\ref{sec:model-floor}; in particular, its physical stationary carrier
$e(-ne^d)$ is not discarded.  Let $P_T$ be the positive reference model and
$\Gfr_T$ its reference Gram.  For a deterministic sequence $\lambda_T>0$ put
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\mathcal D_T:=\Gfr_T^{-1/2}
 (A_T^{\rm true}-\lambda_TP_T)\Gfr_T^{-1/2},
\qquad
\beta_-(T;\lambda_T):=
\frac{\tr[(\mathcal D_T)_-^2]}
{(\kappa_*\lambda_T)^2N_T}.}
\label{eq:beta-minus-intro}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Conditional density one from the one-$\chi$ defect]
\label{thm:main}
Assume that the reference model satisfies
\[
P_T\succeq\kappa_*\,\Gfr_T
\]
on the retained space and that, for some deterministic $\lambda_T$ satisfying
$\lambda_T\ge\lambda_*>0$,
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\beta_-(T;\lambda_T)=O(L^{-2}).}
\label{eq:beta-minus-hypothesis-intro}
\end{equation}
Assume also that the true contour matrix has the decomposition
\[
A_F=A_T^{\rm true}+E_T^{\rm geom}+R_T^{\rm reg},
\]
where $\rank E_T^{\rm geom}=o(N_T)$ and
\begin{equation}
\left\|\Gfr_T^{-1/2}R_T^\nu\Gfr_T^{-1/2}\right\|_{S_2}^2=o(N_T)
\quad(\nu\in\{\mathrm{geom},f',\mathrm{hor}\}),
\label{eq:regular-lambda-scale-intro}
\end{equation}
Then, as $T\to\infty$,
\[
\boxed{\frac{N_{0,T}}{N_T}=1-O(L^{-2})-o(1)=1-o(1)}.
\]
Consequently, by dyadic summation,
\[
\boxed{\frac{N_0(T)}{N(T)}\longrightarrow1}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[Scale of the regular remainder]
The Hilbert--Schmidt triangle inequality and $\lambda_T\ge\lambda_*>0$ turn
the three estimates in \eqref{eq:regular-lambda-scale-intro} into
$\|\Gfr_T^{-1/2}R_T^{\rm reg}\Gfr_T^{-1/2}\|_{S_2}^2
=o(\lambda_T^2N_T)$.  Thus the scaled bound is a conclusion, not an additional
hypothesis.  Since $L\to\infty$, the bound
$\beta_-(T;\lambda_T)=O(L^{-2})$ is in particular $o(1)$.
\end{remark}

Hardy's theorem established infinitely many zeros on the critical line, and
Selberg proved that a positive proportion lie there
\cite{Hardy1914,Selberg1942,Titchmarsh}.  The modern quantitative line begins
with Levinson's mollifier method and its later refinements
\cite{Levinson1974,Young2010,Conrey1989,BCY2011,Feng2012,
PrattRobles2018,PrattRoblesZaharescuZeindler2020}.  Closely related work on
simple and distinct zeros, pair correlation, and finite-dimensional positivity
includes
\cite{HeathBrown1979,Anderson1983,Montgomery1973,ConreyGhoshGonek1998,
BuiHeathBrown2013,ChirreGoncalvesDeLaat2020,Aryan2022,
BaluyotGoldstonSuriajayaTurnage2024,GoldstonSuriajaya2025,
GoldstonSuriajaya2026,AlpogeFurman2026}.  In particular, Alp\"oge and Furman
obtained an unconditional proportion exceeding two thirds for zeros that are
both simple and on the critical line by combining a finite compression of a
Hermitian zero form with inertia.

A second strand concerns the size and statistics of $\zeta$, its derivatives,
and the Hardy $Z$-function.  Mean-value and discrete-moment methods were developed
through the work of Gonek, Conrey--Ghosh, Conrey--Ghosh--Gonek, Ng,
Garunk\v{s}tis--Steuding, Hughes, Milinovich, and later authors
\cite{Gonek1984,ConreyGhosh1985,ConreyGhosh1989,Conrey1988,
ConreyGhosh1998,GarunkstisSteuding2005,Ng2007,Hughes2002,
Milinovich2010,Milinovich2011,HughesMartinPearceCrump2024,
HughesPearceCrump2025,PearceCrump2025,HLP}.  These results are especially close
to the present zero-side use of stationary points of the Hardy gauge and to the
right-edge expansion through $Z_1'/Z_1$.

The arithmetic side also intersects the extensive literature on twisted moments,
Dirichlet-polynomial mean values, mollifiers, and ratios of $L$-functions
\cite{BalasubramanianConreyHeathBrown1985,Farmer1993,
DeshouillersIwaniec1987,BettinChandee2018,BettinChandeeRadziwill2017,
BettinGonek2017,ConreyFarmerKeatingRubinsteinSnaith2003,CFKRS2005,
ConreyFarmerZirnbauer2008,ConreySnaith2007,ConreyGonek2001,
ConreyIwaniecSoundararajan2013,Sono2018,HLZ}.  Adjacent work on zero gaps,
derivative zeros, and critical-line geometry includes
\cite{Conrey1983,Bui2011,BuiMilinovichNg2010,BuiMilinovich2018,
FengWu2012,ChaubeyMalikRoblesZaharescu2018,Ivic1977,KeatingSnaith2000,
Kirila2018,Odlyzko1987,RudnickSarnak1996}.  The argument below uses a different
assembly: the same Hardy-gauge contour matrix is represented on the zero side
and on the packetized right edge, the true log-only operator is compared
spectrally with a positive reference model, and the remaining terms are
separated into low-rank and relative Hilbert--Schmidt classes.

The proof is organized around the normalized contour matrix introduced in
Section~\ref{sec:common-matrix}, whose zero-side and arithmetic-side
representations are developed in the subsequent sections.  The self source is
removed by the exact holomorphic identity of
Lemma~\ref{lem:holomorphic-self-elimination}.  The conditional theorem compares
the true packet operator directly with the positive reference model through
the one-sided defect \eqref{eq:beta-minus-intro}; no scalar diagonal or
lagwise packet--HLZ reduction is used.  The complete source containing
$\Delta H_{\rm dir}$ is absorbed into $A_T^{\rm true}$ before stationary
normalization; safe-line smallness is not used as an operator estimate.  The
superseded frozen-self, critical-$J$ AFE,
one-sided common-output, HLP-resummation, and macroscopic-shift source files are
excluded from the active manuscript.  Appendix~\ref{sec:legacy-route-note}
records only their historical scope; the detailed earlier text remains
recoverable from the Git history.
