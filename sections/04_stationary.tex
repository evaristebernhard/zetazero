\section{The physical \texorpdfstring{one-$\chi$}{one-chi} carrier and exact dilation covariance}
\label{sec:stationary}

A right-edge monomial with total Dirichlet ratio
\[
\xi=\frac{m}{k}
\]
occurs inside a one-$\chi$ integral. After a logarithmic packet displacement
$w$, put $\eta=e^w\xi$. The principal oscillatory kernel is
\[
K_w(\xi)
=\frac1{2\pi}\int A(t)\chi(1-c-it)\eta^{-c-it}\,dt.
\]
Stirling gives
\[
\chi(1-c-it)\eta^{-c-it}
=\left(\frac{t}{2\pi}\right)^{c-1/2}\eta^{-c}
 e^{i\Phi_\eta(t)}(1+O(t^{-1})),
\]
with
\[
\Phi_\eta(t)=t\log\frac{t}{2\pi\eta}-t-\frac\pi4.
\]
Hence
\[
\Phi_\eta'(t)=\log\frac{t}{2\pi\eta},
\qquad
t_*=2\pi\eta,
\qquad
\Phi_\eta''(t_*)=\frac1{t_*}.
\]
At the saddle,
\[
\left(\frac{t_*}{2\pi}\right)^{c-1/2}\eta^{-c}=\eta^{-1/2},
\]
whereas the Gaussian Hessian factor is
\[
\sqrt{\frac{2\pi}{\Phi_\eta''(t_*)}}
=\sqrt{2\pi t_*}=2\pi\eta^{1/2}.
\]
Thus the powers of $\eta$ cancel exactly. The stationary phase
$+\pi/4$ cancels the $-\pi/4$ in $\Phi$, and
\[
e^{i\Phi_\eta(t_*)}e^{i\pi/4}=e^{-2\pi i\eta}=e(-\eta).
\]
Consequently
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
K_w(\xi)=A(2\pi e^w\xi)e(-e^w\xi)+E_w(\xi).
}
\label{eq:stationary-dilation}
\end{equation}
There is no order-one pair-dependent Hessian weight.

If $D_wF(\xi):=F(e^w\xi)$, the principal stationary map satisfies
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\mathcal S_w^{\rm pr}=D_w\mathcal S_0^{\rm pr}.}
\label{eq:dilation-covariance}
\end{equation}

A smooth height cutoff transforms by the same dilation:
\[
\omega\!\left(\frac{2\pi\xi}{T}\right)
\mapsto
\omega\!\left(\frac{2\pi e^w\xi}{T}\right).
\]
Thus the phase, the saddle cutoff, and the principal amplitude are all carried
by the same exact dilation. The higher stationary coefficients, Stirling
corrections, and nonstationary leakage belong to the regular classes estimated
in Proposition~\ref{prop:regular-ledger}. The packet centres in the two
horizontal collars are treated separately by the coordinate restriction
$V_{\rm end}$ of Lemma~\ref{lem:horizontal-decay}; they do not contribute an
edge or regular source term.

On a smooth height block of scale $H=T/L^D$, write $t=t_*v$ on the stationary
sector. There $t_*\asymp T$ uniformly in the shell and arithmetic indices, and
the rescaled saddle $v=1$ remains in a fixed compact subset of the block after
the smooth stationary/nonstationary partition. Consequently every fixed-order
stationary expansion is uniform in the retained indices. Differentiating the
slow block amplitude contributes only fixed combinations of
\[
T^{-1},\qquad H^{-1}=L^D/T,
\qquad T/H^2=L^{2D}/T,
\]
and further fixed derivatives give the same type of $T^{-1}$-times-polylog
bounds. These terms therefore remain in the regular symbol class after the
fixed derivative orders used below.

\begin{lemma}[Uniform smooth stationary symbol]
\label{lem:stationary-symbol}
Fix integers $r,s\ge0$ and a bounded curvature range $|p|\le P$. After the
universal carrier $e(-my)$ is extracted, every direct stationary one-$\chi$
source occurring in the ordinary Hardy term, the summed exact HLP hierarchy,
the translated/coalescing faces, and the fixed Stirling/Hardy corrections has
on a retained unit shell $I_j=[J_j,J_j+1]$ the form
\[
c_m a_{m,T}(y;p)e(-my),
\]
with
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\sup_{y\in I_j,\ |p|\le P}
|\partial_y^r\partial_p^s a_{m,T}(y;p)|
\ll_{r,s,P}L^{C_{r,s}}J_j^{-r}.
}
\label{eq:stationary-symbol-bound}
\end{equation}
The constants are uniform over the retained arithmetic range and over the
summed geometric levels.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Call a height factor $g$ a symbol of order $R$ if
\[
\sup_{0\le q\le R}t^q|g^{(q)}(t)|\ll_R L^C
\]
on $t\asymp T$. This class is closed under finite products and, when a factor
is bounded below by a negative power of $L$, under reciprocal. Stirling's
formula shows that
\[
\lambda(t),\quad f(t)/L,\quad e^{p\lambda(t)},\quad
 e^{\pm2\lambda(t)},\quad t^{\pm2/L}
\]
and every fixed retained gamma correction are symbols, uniformly for bounded
$p$. The $f'$ term gains an additional factor $T^{-1}$.

For the exact HLP hierarchy, level $k$ carries a factor $f(t)^{-k}$ and a
coefficient supported only when $2^k\le m$ by
Lemma~\ref{lem:alpha-identity}. On stationary support $t_*=2\pi my\asymp T$,
so $\log m=O(L)$ and hence every contributing $k$ is $O(L)$. A fixed number of
height derivatives of $f^{-k}$ introduces at most a fixed polynomial in $k$
divided by the corresponding power of $f$; since $k/f=O(1)$ and the
coefficient square sums have the factorial majorant
\eqref{eq:factorial-majorant}, termwise differentiation and summation are
uniformly legitimate. Thus the complete exact HLP amplitude remains in a
fixed polylogarithmic height-symbol class.

For one stationary monomial scale $t=t_*v$. After the exact Hessian
cancellation already proved above, the phase is $t_*\Psi(v)$ with
$\Psi'(1)=0$, $\Psi''(1)\ne0$, and uniformly bounded higher derivatives near
$v=1$. Fixed-order stationary phase therefore produces a finite expansion
whose coefficients are height symbols, with a remainder
$O(T^{-M}L^{C_M})$ after any prescribed fixed order $M$.
Finally,
\[
\partial_y^r g(2\pi my)
=(2\pi m)^rg^{(r)}(2\pi my).
\]
On the shell $my\asymp T$ and $y\asymp J_j$, so
\[
\frac mT\asymp\frac1{J_j}.
\]
The height-symbol bound therefore gives
\[
|\partial_y^r g(2\pi my)|
\ll L^CJ_j^{-r}.
\]
The same argument is uniform after the fixed number of $p$-derivatives. This
proves \eqref{eq:stationary-symbol-bound}.
\end{proof}
