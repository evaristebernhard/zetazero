\section{The physical \texorpdfstring{one-$\chi$}{one-chi} carrier and exact dilation covariance}
\label{sec:stationary}

A right-edge monomial with positive Dirichlet carrier $\xi$ occurs inside a
one-$\chi$ integral.  After a logarithmic packet displacement $w$, put
$\eta=e^w\xi$. The principal oscillatory kernel is
\[
K_w(\xi)
=\frac1{2\pi}\int A(t)\chi(1-c-it)\eta^{-c-it}\,dt.
\]
Stirling gives
\[
\chi(1-c-it)\eta^{-c-it}
=\left(\frac{t}{2\pi}\right)^{c-1/2}\eta^{-c}
 e^{i\Phi_\eta(t)}(1+O(t^{-1})),
\]
with
\[
\Phi_\eta(t)=t\log\frac{t}{2\pi\eta}-t-\frac\pi4.
\]
Hence
\[
\Phi_\eta'(t)=\log\frac{t}{2\pi\eta},
\qquad
t_*=2\pi\eta,
\qquad
\Phi_\eta''(t_*)=\frac1{t_*}.
\]
At the saddle,
\[
\left(\frac{t_*}{2\pi}\right)^{c-1/2}\eta^{-c}=\eta^{-1/2},
\]
whereas the Gaussian Hessian factor is
\[
\sqrt{\frac{2\pi}{\Phi_\eta''(t_*)}}
=\sqrt{2\pi t_*}=2\pi\eta^{1/2}.
\]
Thus the powers of $\eta$ cancel exactly. The stationary phase $+\pi/4$
cancels the $-\pi/4$ in $\Phi$, and
\[
e^{i\Phi_\eta(t_*)}e^{i\pi/4}=e^{-2\pi i\eta}=e(-\eta).
\]
Consequently
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
K_w(\xi)=A(2\pi e^w\xi)e(-e^w\xi)+E_w(\xi).
}
\label{eq:stationary-dilation}
\end{equation}
There is no order-one pair-dependent Hessian weight.

For completeness, the oscillatory Gaussian constant is obtained without
formal continuation of a positive-real-part Gaussian.  One integration by
parts gives, for $0<R\le S$,
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\left|\int_R^S e^{iy^2/2}\,dy\right|\le\frac2R.
}
\label{eq:fresnel-tail}
\end{equation}
Apply the ordinary Gaussian formula to the Abel-damped kernel
$e^{-(\varepsilon-i/2)y^2}$ and use the same uniform tail estimate.  Dominated
convergence on fixed windows and then $\varepsilon\downarrow0$ give
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\lim_{R\to\infty}\int_{-R}^R e^{iy^2/2}\,dy
=\sqrt\pi(1+i),
\qquad
\left|\int_{-R}^R e^{iy^2/2}\,dy-\sqrt\pi(1+i)\right|
\le\frac4R.
}
\label{eq:fresnel-window}
\end{equation}

If $D_wF(\xi):=F(e^w\xi)$, the principal stationary map satisfies
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\mathcal S_w^{\rm pr}=D_w\mathcal S_0^{\rm pr}.}
\label{eq:dilation-covariance}
\end{equation}
A smooth height cutoff transforms by the same exact dilation.  Thus the phase,
saddle cutoff and principal amplitude all carry the same packet lag.  Higher
stationary coefficients, Stirling corrections and nonstationary leakage are
estimated by the fixed-order symbol and integration-by-parts arguments below
and in Sections~\ref{sec:shell-frame}--\ref{sec:logonly-remainder-ledger}.

On a smooth height block of scale $H=T/L^D$, write $t=t_*v$ on the stationary
sector. There $t_*\asymp T$ uniformly, and the rescaled saddle $v=1$ remains in
a fixed compact subset after the smooth stationary/nonstationary partition.
Every fixed-order stationary expansion is therefore uniform.  Differentiating
the slow block amplitude contributes only fixed combinations of
\[
T^{-1},\qquad H^{-1}=L^D/T,
\qquad T/H^2=L^{2D}/T,
\]
and further fixed derivatives remain $T^{-1}$ times a fixed polylogarithmic
factor.

\begin{lemma}[Uniform smooth stationary symbol]
\label{lem:stationary-symbol}
Fix integers $r,s\ge0$ and a bounded curvature range $|p|\le P$.  After the
universal carrier is extracted, every stationary monomial belonging to the
exact arithmetic source $H_{\rm ar}$, the $f'$-regular family, and the fixed
Stirling/Hardy corrections has on a retained
unit shell $I_j=[J_j,J_j+1]$ the form
\[
c_m a_{m,T}(y;p)e(-my)
\]
with
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\sup_{y\in I_j,\ |p|\le P}
|\partial_y^r\partial_p^s a_{m,T}(y;p)|
\ll_{r,s,P}L^{C_{r,s}}J_j^{-r}.
}
\label{eq:stationary-symbol-bound}
\end{equation}
If the source carries an explicit $f'$ factor, the right side has in addition
a factor $T^{-1}$ before the fixed polylogarithmic loss.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Call a height factor $g$ a symbol of order $R$ if
\[
\sup_{0\le q\le R}t^q|g^{(q)}(t)|\ll_R L^C
\]
on $t\asymp T$. This class is closed under finite products and, when a factor
is bounded below by a negative power of $L$, under reciprocal. Stirling's
formula shows that
\[
\lambda(t),\quad f(t)/L,\quad e^{p\lambda(t)},\quad
 e^{\pm2\lambda(t)},\quad t^{\pm2/L}
\]
and every fixed retained gamma correction are symbols, uniformly for bounded
$p$.  The reciprocal $(f-P)^{-1}$ is $O(L^{-1})$ on the safe line and its fixed
height derivatives remain symbols.  The $f'$ class gains $T^{-1}$.

For $H_{\rm ar}$ the translated coefficients are uniformly bounded by fixed
logarithmic losses on every physical range $m\le TL^{C_x}$, and fixed curvature
derivatives contribute only powers of $\log m/L=O(1)$.  No safe-line
$O(L^{-2})$ bound for $\Delta H_{\rm dir}$ is used after stationary phase.

For one stationary monomial set $t=t_*v$. After the exact Hessian cancellation
proved above, the phase is $t_*\Psi(v)$ with
$\Psi'(1)=0$, $\Psi''(1)\ne0$, and uniformly bounded higher derivatives near
$v=1$.  Fixed-order stationary phase gives a finite expansion whose
coefficients are height symbols, with a remainder
$O(T^{-M}L^{C_M})$ after any prescribed fixed order $M$.
Finally,
\[
\partial_y^r g(2\pi my)
=(2\pi m)^rg^{(r)}(2\pi my).
\]
On the shell $my\asymp T$ and $y\asymp J_j$, so
$m/T\asymp J_j^{-1}$.  The symbol bound therefore gives
\[
|\partial_y^r g(2\pi my)|\ll L^CJ_j^{-r}.
\]
The same argument is uniform after the fixed $p$-derivatives, proving
\eqref{eq:stationary-symbol-bound}.
\end{proof}
