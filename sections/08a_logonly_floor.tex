\subsection{Log-only frozen model and certified spectral floor}
\label{sec:logonly-model-floor}

The revised principal source contains only the two logarithmic faces of
\eqref{eq:Hlog}.  Accordingly the frozen profile is the function
$G_{\log}$ of \eqref{eq:Glog-from-source}, not the earlier profile containing
the critical-$J$ self contribution.

A direct coefficient extraction from \eqref{eq:Glog-from-source} gives
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
G_{\log}(\rho)
=-\frac76\rho^4+2\rho^3-\rho^2
+\frac{e^2}{4}\left[
(2\rho^2-4\rho+3)\sinh^2\rho-\rho^2
\right].
}
\label{eq:Glog-closed}
\end{equation}
Equivalently this is the old completed profile with
$G_{\rm self}(\rho)=\rho/2-\rho^2+(2/3)\rho^3$ deleted before the principal
operator is formed.

Define
\[
K_{G_{\log}}(r,s):=G_{\log}(1-|r-s|)
\]
and let $A_T^{G_{\log}}$ be its pullback through the normalized stable-core
packet analysis of Corollary~\ref{cor:frozen-logonly-lag-kernel}.  Define the
reference form $\Gfr_T$ by the same triangular primitive map as in
\eqref{eq:global-reference-Gram}.

\begin{proposition}[Certified log-only spectral floor]
\label{prop:logonly-model-floor}
On every mean-zero stable core fibre,
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
K_{G_{\log}}\succeq0.02\,Q_\triangle.
}
\label{eq:logonly-model-floor-fibre}
\end{equation}
Consequently
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
A_T^{G_{\log}}\succeq0.02\,\Gfr_T.
}
\label{eq:logonly-model-floor}
\end{equation}
No additional model spectral deletion is required.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Extend a core input by zero to $[0,1]$.  Put
$q=Q'$ with $Q(0)=Q(1)=0$ and let
\[
g_{\log}(r):=G_{\log}''(r).
\]
The primitive identity \eqref{eq:primitive-identity} gives
\begin{equation}
\langle K_{G_{\log}}q,q\rangle
=a_{\log}\|Q\|_2^2
-\iint g_{\log}(1-|x-y|)Q(x)\overline{Q(y)}\,dx\,dy,
\label{eq:logonly-primitive}
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
a_{\log}=2G_{\log}'(1)>4.6771.
}
\label{eq:logonly-cusp}
\end{equation}
Differentiating \eqref{eq:Glog-closed} gives an explicit elementary
combination of polynomials, $e^2$, $\sinh r$ and $\cosh r$.  We certify the
Schur bound by the same positive Taylor enclosure
\eqref{eq:exp-rational-bound} used in Proposition~\ref{prop:model-floor}.
With $m=64$, exact rational arithmetic gives
\begin{equation}
\begin{gathered}
g_{\log}(0.2483)>0.0010,\qquad
g_{\log}(0.2484)<-0.0002,\\
g_{\log}(0.6464)<-0.0001,\qquad
g_{\log}(0.6465)>0.0014,\\
G_{\log}^{(4)}(r)>58\qquad(0\le r\le1),\\
g_{\log}(0.3144)+g_{\log}(0.6856)>0.0018,\\
g_{\log}(0.3145)+g_{\log}(0.6855)<-0.0010,\\
2.3385<G_{\log}'(1)<2.3386,\\
0.5703<G_{\log}'(a)<0.5704,\qquad
0.2024<G_{\log}'(b)<0.2025,\\
G_{\log}'(0.3144)<0.5453,\qquad
G_{\log}'(0.6855)>0.2158,
\end{gathered}
\label{eq:Glog-rational-certificates}
\end{equation}
where $a\in(0.2483,0.2484)$ and
$b\in(0.6464,0.6465)$ are the two zeros of $g_{\log}$.
The bound $G_{\log}^{(4)}>58$ makes $g_{\log}$ strictly convex, hence these
are its only two zeros.

For
\[
M_{\log}(x):=\int_0^1
|g_{\log}(1-|x-y|)|\,dy,
\]
symmetry reduces the maximum to $0\le x\le1/2$.  The same sign-pattern
argument as in Proposition~\ref{prop:model-floor} shows that the unique
maximizer lies in $(0.3144,0.3145)$: on the middle interval its derivative is
$g_{\log}(x)+g_{\log}(1-x)$, whose sign change is certified in
\eqref{eq:Glog-rational-certificates}; strict convexity handles the two outer
intervals.  Integrating $|g_{\log}|$ by the antiderivative
$G_{\log}'$ and using the same rational enclosures gives the conservative
bound
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\sup_{x\in[0,1]}M_{\log}(x)<4.602.
}
\label{eq:Glog-Schur}
\end{equation}
Together with \eqref{eq:logonly-cusp}, Schur's test in
\eqref{eq:logonly-primitive} yields
\[
\langle K_{G_{\log}}q,q\rangle
>(4.6771-4.602)\|Q\|_2^2
>0.075\|Q\|_2^2
>0.02Q_\triangle(q).
\]
The packet statement follows by congruence through the common stable-core
analysis map supplied directly by the lagwise bridge.  No one-sided arithmetic
multiplier is used in this pullback.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Numerical margin]
The certified constants above are deliberately coarse.  High-precision
numerics locate the two zeros near $0.24837895$ and $0.64640709$ and the Schur
maximum near $x=0.3145$, with a resulting floor about
$0.0377Q_\triangle$.  Only the rigorous $0.02$ bound is used below.
\end{remark}

For the revised proof we henceforth write
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
P_T:=A_T^{G_{\log}},\qquad c_0:=0.02.
}
\label{eq:PT-logonly-definition}
\end{equation}
Thus any later invocation of the abstract min--max lemma uses the log-only
principal operator and the constant $c_0=0.02$.
