# Closure Audit: Exact Pre-HLZ Packet Separation for the Finite-Model Principal Source

Date: 2026-09-03

## Scope

This note audits the theorem-sized A2 interface in the density-one proof:

\[
\text{physical packetized finite-model source}
\longrightarrow
\text{one common scalar-HLZ output channel}.
\]

The relevant manuscript statement is Lemma `lem:pre-HLZ-packet-separation`.

The question is whether, before the HLZ Mellin contour is moved and before the finite physical-shift Cauchy residues are taken, all packet-label dependence really factors through one analysis map independent of the scalar parameters \((u,\mathbf z,p)\).

## 1. Exact packet double-Fourier identity

The packet polarization is evaluated at the unshifted central contour point. For basis packets,

\[
\psi_{L,\tau}(z)=\int \phi_L(x)e^{ix(z-\tau)}\,dx,
\]

and with \(s=c+it\), \(\sigma_0=c-1/2\), one obtains exactly

\[
\mathcal B_{e_r,e_s}(c+it)
=
\iint
\phi_L(x)\phi_L(y)
 e^{-i\tau_sx+i\tau_ry}
 e^{\sigma_0(x-y)}e^{it(x-y)}\,dx\,dy.
\]

Thus packet labels occur only in the two separated phases

\[
e^{-i\tau_sx},\qquad e^{i\tau_ry}.
\]

Neither the physical curvature parameter \(p\) nor the later HLZ Cauchy variables \(\mathbf z\) enter the packet legs.

## 2. Exact lag reduction for each scalar one-chi carrier

For one scalar Dirichlet monomial \(\xi^{-c-it}\), put

\[
d=y-x.
\]

Then

\[
e^{\sigma_0(x-y)}e^{it(x-y)}\xi^{-c-it}
=
e^{d/2}(\xi e^d)^{-c-it}.
\]

This identity is exact. Therefore, apart from the two packet phases, the one-\(\chi\) integrand depends on \((x,y)\) only through the difference \(d\).

The stationary phase has

\[
\eta=\xi e^d,
\qquad
t_*=2\pi\eta,
\]

and the principal Hessian normalization cancels the power of \(\eta\). Hence principal stationary reduction generates no dependence on \(x+y\) or on either packet leg separately.

All retained archimedean, physical-shift, Cauchy-shift, translated-face and frozen-block factors are scalar. Evaluated at the saddle they remain functions of \(d\) and scalar parameters only.

## 3. Absolute convergence of the translated finite-model face

A possible hidden issue was the translated term

\[
P(s-2/L).
\]

If the right edge had been only \(1+O(1/L)\), its translated Dirichlet series could have left the absolute-convergence half-plane.

However the invariant contour fixes

\[
\boxed{c=2}.
\]

Therefore on the right edge

\[
\Re(s-2/L)=2-2/L>1
\]

for all sufficiently large \(T\).

Consequently both

\[
P(s),\qquad P(s-2/L)
\]

and the finite products used in the frozen finite model are absolutely convergent on the original contour. The summation of scalar Dirichlet monomials is therefore legitimate before any analytic continuation or HLZ contour movement.

On the compact packet-difference range generated by \(\operatorname{supp}\phi_L\), this gives a bounded smooth lag kernel with only fixed polylogarithmic derivative losses.

## 4. Physical-shift derivatives do not create packet-map jets

The manuscript already proves a uniform stationary-symbol lemma for bounded physical curvature parameter \(p\):

\[
\sup |\partial_y^r\partial_p^s a_{m,T}(y;p)|
\ll L^{C_{r,s}}J^{-r}.
\]

Therefore differentiation in \(p\) acts only on the scalar stationary amplitude. It does not alter the two packet phases or the Fourier packet legs.

After Fourier diagonalization of the lag convolution operator one may take

\[
\mathscr C_Te_s=\mathcal F a_{s,T},
\]

with \(\mathscr C_T\) independent of

\[
(u,\mathbf z,p).
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\partial_p\mathscr C_T=0,
\qquad
\partial_{z_j}\mathscr C_T=0.
}
\]

All such derivatives act on the common output multiplier.

## 5. Moving packet support and transition caps

The principal frozen source uses a fixed central flat-core restriction of each packet leg. This restriction is parameter-independent.

The complementary pieces are exactly:

- central--exterior;
- exterior--central;
- exterior--exterior,

and are assigned to Proposition `prop:exterior-packet-support`.

That proposition explicitly includes:

- both fixed-width transition caps;
- core--exterior cross terms;
- tail--exterior cross terms;
- low-rank stationary exterior rows;
- relative-HS nonstationary exterior leakage.

The true packet-support endpoints have vanishing jets because the window is \(C_c^\infty\). Artificial interfaces are assigned to the existing boundary and terminal-tail edge ledgers.

Hence the flat-core split does not create an unowned packet-map endpoint derivative.

## 6. Common-output conclusion

For the finite-model principal source the pre-HLZ packet form is exactly

\[
\mathcal Q_{rs}^{\rm preHLZ}(u,\mathbf z,p)
=
\iint
 a_{s,T}(x)\overline{a_{r,T}(y)}
 \kappa_T(y-x;u,\mathbf z,p)\,dx\,dy.
\]

Fourier transform diagonalizes the lag operator, giving

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal Q_{rs}^{\rm preHLZ}
=
\langle
M_{u,\mathbf z,p}\mathscr C_Te_s,
\mathscr C_Te_r
\rangle,
}
\]

with one \(\mathscr C_T\) independent of all scalar completion variables.

The scalar HLZ contour shift is linear in the multiplier, so the same output channel is preserved for both the principal residue and scalar contour error. The later reference-remainder section converts this multiplier bound to a relative-HS estimate without packet-dimension loss.
